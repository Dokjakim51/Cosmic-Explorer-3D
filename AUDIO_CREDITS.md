# Cosmic Explorer — Ambient Audio Credits

## Distribution status

Phase 8.14 includes **234 original audio files** rendered specifically for Cosmic Explorer: 117 OGG/Opus files and 117 MP3 compatibility fallbacks. They contain three stems for each of the 39 sound identities:

- relaxing music;
- artistic ambience;
- data-inspired sonification.

No third-party recording, copyrighted song, NASA/ESA audio file, streaming URL, or sample library is redistributed. The files were synthesized locally by `scripts/generate-ambient-assets.py` from original oscillator, chord, noise, pulse, sweep, and envelope recipes.

- identities: **39**;

- runtime internet dependency: **none**;
- attribution required for embedded recordings: **none**, because no external recording is included.

## Classification

- **Relaxing music** — original slow harmonic compositions generated separately for every identity.
- **Artistic ambience** — original textures inspired by the place or phenomenon.
- **Data-inspired sonification** — original pulse and sweep patterns inspired by astronomical behavior. These are not official scientific data products and are not literal recordings from space.
